TTC buses, metro and cable cars · Tbilisi

Your next bus, one tap away.

Save the routes you actually ride. Gzaap shows the next departure from your stop with live predictions, keeps timetables for when the signal drops, and puts the buses on a map.

Free · no account · no ads · no tracking

Built around your trips, not the whole network

Most of the time you need one answer: when is my bus? Gzaap keeps that answer on the first screen.

Your trips first

A trip is a route, a direction and your stop. Combine routes that both work, like 380 and 316, into one list, and flip to the way back with one tap.

Live online, ready offline

Live arrival predictions from TTC when you are connected. Saved timetables keep working on the road with no signal.

Buses on the map

See where each bus on your route is right now, with your boarding stops marked.

Timetable for any day

Today, tomorrow or any date from the calendar, with the days TTC has confirmed marked.

Home Screen widgetsphone app

Android and iPhone widgets show the next departure without opening the app.

Your language

English, Georgian or Russian, with light and dark themes and a choice of color palettes.

One trip, three views

A new trip in three taps

  1. Pick a route

    Any TTC bus, metro line or cable car.

  2. Choose the direction

    Where you are heading on that route.

  3. Pick your stop

    Nearest stops come first. Gzaap then suggests other routes from that stop and offers to add the way back.

Get Gzaap

Your trips stay on your device. Nothing to sign up for.

Any phone or computer

Web app

Runs in the browser. Add it to your Home Screen and it opens like an app, offline timetables included.

iPhone: open in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Android and computers: use the browser's Install option.
